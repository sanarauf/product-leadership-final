# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Field superintendents and foreman input their data (photos, texts) in Meridian and use that as the primary method for project management and collaboration.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Field superintendents and foremen from the 38 of the top 100 US construction firms where Meridian is system of record.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Meridian is already the system of record for 38 of the top 100 US construction firms. Focus on becoming the source of truth for field men for projects at those 38 firms.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Creating user flows that are intuitive that the target user can seamlessly transition to using. Understand why target users are defaulting to off-app methods (what is missing).
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Weekly Active Field Contributor Rate (% of field roles logging data daily), Field-to-RFI Resolution Cycle Time, and Off-App Email/SMS Attachment Volume

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** To win field mindshare against SMS and photo galleries, the mobile app must remain exclusively optimized for frictionless data capture and instant messaging (under-15-second interactions). Adding administrative governance, approval gates, or multi-field enterprise forms onto job-site surfaces creates cognitive burden, slows down mobile performance, and reproduces the exact bureaucratic friction that forced superintendents and foremen to default to informal text chains in the first place.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Transform Meridian into the frictionless, primary daily entry point for frontline field teams, making field-captured data the real-time source of truth for back-office operations.
- **KR1:** Increase the percentage of weekly active field superintendents and foremen at our 38 enterprise firms who log at least one field update (photo, daily log, or issue) directly in Meridian.
- **KR2:** Reduce the average lag time between a field photo/observation being captured and its formal logging in backoffice RFIs or daily reports.
- **KR3:** Decrease the volume of backoffice-initiated manual data entries and offline text/email attachments processed per active project.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** We are assuming that "intuition" and "understanding what is missing" are our primary blockers, while ignoring the core operational differentiator we claimed. We state in How to Win that your differentiator is merely being the existing system of record at 38 firms. If those 38 firms already mandate Meridian, but foremen still use WhatsApp to avoid formal paper trails or speed bumps, making the app smoother won't force compliance. We assume field workers want to feed the enterprise system if it's easier, when they may actively prefer the informal, unmonitored cover of a group text.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I would interview the field workers to better understand their user preferences. I want to identify if the resistance to adopting Meridian is due to a structural issue, missing functionality, or another reason. Uncovering the root reason why they navigate to device camera / messages instead of the Meridian platform will help us understand what is driving that decision.
